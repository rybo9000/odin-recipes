Cap'n Crunch Spaghetti

Description

Ooey Gooey Spaghetti with crunchy flavor bursts of Cap'n Crunch cereal will make your taste buds explode with delight!

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Sauce
  • Cap'n Crunch Cereal
  • Parmesan Cheese

Steps

  1. Moisten the noodles and make them limp
  2. Add sauce
  3. Garnish with Cap'n Crunch & Cheese
