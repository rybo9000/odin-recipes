Skittle Grilled Cheese

Description

This recipe delights the inner child in all of us by combining savory and sweet into a gooey crunchy ball of love.

Ingredients

  • Skittles
  • Cheese
  • Bread
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Slather Butter on the Bread
  2. Add Cheese & Skittles
  3. Grill for five minutes
